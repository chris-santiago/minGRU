# Tutorials

Hands-on lessons that take you from an empty environment to working models. Do them in order; each step produces visible output.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install the package and train your first minGRU stack.
- [Two-layer stacks](two-layer-stacks.md): compose heterogeneous mixers in the right order for your problem.
- [Your first delta model](first-delta-model.md): build and train a `DeltaMinGRU` composer, learn its `(y_t, h_t)` step return, and turn on time decay.
- [Triton on GPU](triton-on-gpu.md): enable the Triton scan kernels and verify they engage.
